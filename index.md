# Privacy Policy for NerveTrader

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026  
**Application:** NerveTrader (`app.nervetrader.android`)  
**Contact:** `semenasha12345@gmail.com`

This Privacy Policy explains how NerveTrader ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Educational Simulation Only (No Real Money)

NerveTrader is an educational trading psychology and market simulation game. All charts are synthetically generated and all balances use virtual currency. The App does **not** connect to brokerage accounts, does **not** execute real trades, does **not** process payments or banking information, and does **not** provide personalized financial or investment advice.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We do not require account registration, login, name, or email address to use the App.

The following gameplay data is stored **strictly locally on your device** (using Android `SharedPreferences`, with cloud backup disabled):
- Completed levels, lessons, quiz scores, and exercise progress
- Virtual balance, XP, daily streak, and local history of simulated trades

This local data is never sent to our own servers. You can erase all local data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App.

---

## 3. Automatically Collected Diagnostics & Analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand how features are used, fix crashes, and improve stability, the App uses **Google Firebase** services provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited:

- **Firebase Analytics:** collects aggregated, pseudonymous usage events (for example, app opens, level completions, lesson completions, and feature usage), basic device metadata (device model, OS version, app version, language/locale, approximate country/region), and an app-instance identifier. We do **not** send your name, email address, contacts, or real financial information to Firebase Analytics.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** collects crash reports, stack traces, and technical device state (OS version, device model, free memory/storage at crash time) solely to diagnose and fix technical errors.
- **Firebase Remote Config:** fetches non-personal configuration parameters (such as feature flags) so we can safely adjust app behavior without requiring an app update.

Google processes this data in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

---

## 4. Permissions & Device Features

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Optional permission used solely to schedule local daily streak reminders on your device. No push notification token is used for marketing spam. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your Android system settings.
- **Share Feature:** When you tap "Share card" to share your virtual Trader Mindset card, the App generates a PNG image in local cache and hands it to the standard Android share sheet. You choose whether and where to share it; we do not upload the image to any server.

---

## 5. Advertising & Third-Party Links

The current version of NerveTrader does **not** include third-party advertising SDKs. If advertising or optional in-app purchases are introduced in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and Google Play's Data Safety disclosures before releasing that update.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you clear App data or uninstall the App.
- **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics data** is retained according to standard Google Firebase retention periods (typically 90 days for Crashlytics logs and up to 14 months for Analytics event data).

Because we do not collect accounts, names, or email addresses, we cannot identify individual users directly. To exercise your rights or ask any privacy question, contact us at **`semenasha12345@gmail.com`**.
